Who We Are

A Church Gathered by Grace

Sovereign Grace Christian Community is a Reformed evangelical church committed to the authority of Scripture, the centrality of Jesus Christ, and salvation by the grace of God.

We seek to grow together as a worshiping, learning, serving, and witnessing community—trusting the Word of God and depending on His grace in every part of life.

“For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory forever. Amen.”ROMANS 11:36

Our Purpose

Our Vision & Mission

Seeking the glory of God through the gospel of Jesus Christ.

01

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered, Reformed evangelical church where God is worshiped in truth, Scripture is faithfully preached, believers are matured in holiness, families are discipled in the faith, and the gospel of grace is proclaimed to the nations for the glory of God alone.

02

Our Mission

We exist to glorify God by exalting Jesus Christ, faithfully preaching His Word, making mature disciples, cultivating a gospel-centered church family, equipping believers for ministry, and proclaiming the gospel to our community and the nations.

03

Our Commitment

We are committed to Scripture as our final authority and to the historic Christian faith as summarized in the historic Reformed confessions.

Our Foundation

What We Believe

Our faith and practice are governed by the Word of God. These summaries introduce central biblical convictions we seek to teach and live.

The Authority of Scripture

The Bible is God-breathed and is our supreme and sufficient authority for faith and life. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)

The Triune God

There is one true and living God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. (Matthew 28:19)

The Person and Work of Christ

Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, the only Mediator, whose life, death, and resurrection accomplish salvation for His people. (1 Timothy 2:5; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4)

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is God's gracious work, received through faith in Jesus Christ—not earned by human merit. (Ephesians 2:8–10)

The Church and Its Mission

Christ gathers His people into the church to worship, grow in the Word, serve one another, and make disciples. (Matthew 28:18–20; Ephesians 4:11–16)

The Return of Christ

Jesus Christ will return bodily in glory, raise the dead, judge the world, and bring His people into the fullness of eternal life. (Acts 1:11; Revelation 21:1–4)

This is an introductory summary, not a complete statement of faith. The church's formally adopted confession and doctrinal statement should be published here after review and approval.
